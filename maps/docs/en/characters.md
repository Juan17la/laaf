# Characters — LAAF

See [story.md](story.md) for context and [map.md](map.md) for zones.

Each member of the group has: a loss, a zone, a hunting style, a weakness the player can exploit, and a **spare / kill** resolution. Hunters are **persistent enemies**: they can't be killed outside their boss fight — in normal encounters they can only be stunned or escaped.

## 1. Player — Alex Moreno

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Role | Protagonist, outsider |
| Goal | Find sister Lucía and get out of Hollowmere alive |
| Personality | Stubborn, sarcastic under stress, protective |
| Abilities | Sprint, crouch, hide, climb low walls, dodge roll, melee, limited firearms, throwables |
| Arc | From "I just want my sister back" to deciding what kind of person survival makes them |

## 2. The group (the five friends)

### 2.1 Elena Ruiz — "Moth" (the traitor)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 27 |
| Lost | Younger brother Tomás (14) |
| Zone | Harbor & Lighthouse |
| Role before | Radio hobbyist, worked at the harbor with her father |
| Role now | Officially: watches the harbor so nobody leaves by boat. Secretly: informant, codename *Moth* |
| Personality | Calm, sharp, dry humor, carries guilt for surviving |
| Look | Yellow rain jacket, cropped hair, bandaged branded hand (she hides the mark) |

- **Function in gameplay:** guide and quest giver. Talks by radio (channel 7), leaves notes with maps of each zone and hints about the next member's weakness. Hides **evidence pieces** around town.
- **Relationship to player:** starts as using Alex as a tool; grows into real trust. Must feel like a friend by the time she dies.
- **Death:** executed by the group at the Church (Chapter 6). Not preventable. Her radio, jacket and last recording remain in the Lighthouse.
- **Last words:** *"Channel seven… one‑four‑one‑three. Tell them his name was Tomás."* (the code opens the lighthouse transmitter).

### 2.2 Owen Clarke — "The Woodsman"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 26 |
| Lost | Both parents, locked in the burning sawmill |
| Zone | Sawmill & Pinewood Forest |
| Personality | Quiet, obsessive, the most loyal to The Veil because he believes his parents' deaths "must mean something" |
| Weapons | Hunting bow, then chainsaw in the boss fight; places bear traps and tripwire bells |
| Hunting style | Tracker: follows footprints and noise, sets traps on the player's route, whistles to signal others |
| Weakness | **Fire.** He freezes when he sees open flame (trauma). Flares and burning barrels stun him for longer |
| Hunting lines | "I can hear you breathing." / "Dad used to say the forest keeps everything." |
| Spare | He drops the chainsaw near a fire, crying. Gives Alex the key part: "Tell me it wasn't for nothing." |
| Kill | He dies saying his mother's name. Key part taken from his body |

### 2.3 Nora Vance — "The Teacher"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 29 |
| Lost | Daughter Lily (6) |
| Zone | Hollowmere Elementary School |
| Personality | Soft voice, motherly, deeply broken. Talks to Lily as if she were there |
| Weapons | Scissors/letter opener, sedative syringes; uses the PA system and music box |
| Hunting style | Psychological: speaks through the PA, locks doors remotely, leaves music boxes as noise traps, mannequins dressed as children to confuse the player |
| Weakness | **The power room.** Cutting power kills the PA and the locks; in the dark she panics and calls for Lily, revealing her position |
| Hunting lines | "Lily, sweetheart, we have a guest." / "Recess is over. Come back to class." |
| Spare | Alex gives her Lily's drawing (found in the zone). She lets go of the scissors and gives the key part |
| Kill | She dies holding a child's jacket. The school's music box stops |

### 2.4 Julian Park — "The Medic"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 28 |
| Lost | Fiancée Sam |
| Zone | St. Agnes Clinic |
| Personality | Clinical, cold, rational. Justifies everything as "triage": some must be lost so others survive |
| Weapons | Scalpel, tranquilizer gun, sedative gas canisters |
| Hunting style | Fast and precise. Floods rooms with gas, uses security cameras, disables hiding spots he has seen used |
| Weakness | **Ventilation system.** Reversing the vents pushes the gas back to him; he has asthma. Also a **gas mask** can be found in the zone |
| Hunting lines | "Hold still. This is easier if you don't fight it." / "I lost her on this table." |
| Spare | Alex shows him Sam's file (proof The Veil killed her, not an accident). He hands over the key part and his clinic keys |
| Kill | He dies calmly, checking his own pulse. "Time of death…" |

### 2.5 Marcus Hale — "The Deacon"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Age | 31 |
| Lost | Wife Clara |
| Zone | Church & Cemetery |
| Personality | Oldest, leader of the group, protector. Believes he keeps the others alive by obeying. The one who executes Elena |
| Weapons | Sledgehammer, lantern; very tough |
| Hunting style | Slow but relentless. Great hearing. Rings the church bell to call the others. Blocks exits |
| Weakness | **The bells.** Ringing the bell tower from the side passages deafens him for seconds. Heavy attacks leave him open |
| Hunting lines | "I kept them alive. All of them. Until she ruined it." / "Clara sang in this choir." |
| Holds | The **chapel key** to the Veil's Chapel in the quarry |
| Spare | He kneels in front of Clara's grave: "Elena was right. She was always right." Gives the chapel key and joins the finale as an ally |
| Kill | He falls under the bell. The bell rings once more by itself |

## 3. Other characters

### 3.1 The Shepherd (antagonist)
- Only a voice on the radio until the finale. Calm, fatherly, sermon‑like.
- Taunts the player after Elena's death: *"She flew too close to the light, little moth."*
- Revealed in the quarry chapel as an old man with a radio, a ledger and a brand iron. Can be killed, spared or left behind depending on the ending.

### 3.2 Lucía Moreno
- Alex's sister, photographer. Came to photograph the "abandoned festival". Marked and taken.
- Found in the quarry in every ending except **Run**. Her camera (with photos of The Veil) is evidence #11, hidden in her old motel room.

### 3.3 Grady (neutral merchant)
- Old owner of **Grady's General Store** (town square safe house).
- Pays tribute to The Veil and is therefore left alone. Trades with anyone: *"I don't pick sides. I pick prices."*
- Runs the shop and upgrades. Comments on the player's choices (e.g., if a member was killed).

### 3.4 Silas (drunk NPC)
- Regular at **The Drowned Lantern** bar. Plays the card minigame. Lost his son at the festival.
- Gives rumors and small hints when he's winning.

### 3.5 Marked townspeople
- A few other victims marked by the group; can be found hiding. Optional side encounters: give them supplies for tokens/evidence, or ignore them. Some are taken during blackouts if not helped.
