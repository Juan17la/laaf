# Map — Hollowmere

See [characters.md](characters.md) for the members and [minigames.md](minigames.md) for minigame rules.

## 1. Overview

Hollowmere is an isolated valley town surrounded by mountains. It is designed as a **hub‑and‑zones** map: a central, relatively safe town square connected to five zones. **Each zone is owned by one member of the group**, who patrols it and fights the player there.

- **Isolation:** collapsed bridge (south), mountains (east/west), the lake (center‑west), the Old Pass Tunnel gate (north, locked).
- **Size target:** each zone ~10–15 minutes of exploration; hub reachable from every zone in under 2 minutes of walking.
- **Day/night:** exploring by day is safer (members patrol less); at night hunters are active; **blackout at 3:00** each night (see [gameplay.md](gameplay.md)).

## 3. Hub and safe locations

### 3.1 Town Square (hub)
- **Grady's General Store** — safe house: shop, upgrades, save point, storage box.
- **The Drowned Lantern** (bar) — card minigame with Silas; rumors. Beating Silas in a high‑stakes game wins **evidence #12** (a letter from The Shepherd he stole).
- **Festival grounds** — abandoned Lantern Festival stalls; **shooting gallery** minigame; burnt lanterns everywhere (environmental story).
- Hunters **never enter** Grady's store (tribute rule). After Elena's death (Act III), they patrol the square at night.

### 3.2 Lakeview Motel (start)
- Room 6: Alex's room, first save point, the **radio** (radio tuning minigame).
- Room 4: Lucía's old room, locked (lockpicking) — **evidence #11**, Lucía's camera.
- Diner next door: **arcade cabinet** minigame.
- Used as the tutorial zone in Chapter 1 (Owen's first hunt).

### 3.3 Safe rooms
One per zone, marked with a **moth symbol** painted by Elena. They reset the Mark meter partially, allow saving, and hunters don't enter them. After Elena dies, safe rooms stay, but one per zone is **burned** by the group (fewer safe rooms in Act III).

## 4. Zones

Each zone lists: owner, atmosphere, layout, objectives, key items, Elena's clue, minigames, escape routes.

### 4.1 Pinewood Forest & Sawmill — Owner: **Owen**
- **Atmosphere:** fog, tall pines, sound of distant saws, hanging traps, burnt sawmill with his parents' memorial.
- **Layout:** forest trails (open, lots of tall grass to hide) → logging camp (cabins) → sawmill (multi‑level interior, conveyor belts, log piles) → boss arena: burnt sawmill floor with fuel barrels.
- **Objectives:** find the key part (in Owen's cabin safe → lockpicking) or fight Owen.
- **Key items:** Key part 1, flares (fire weakness), his parents' festival lantern (spare condition), evidence #1–2.
- **Elena's clue:** "He can't look at fire. Not since that night."
- **Minigame:** lockpicking (cabins).
- **Escape routes:** river stream (breaks scent/footprints), cabin crawlspaces, the log slide down to the motel.

### 4.2 Hollowmere Elementary School — Owner: **Nora**
- **Atmosphere:** children's drawings (many of Lily), classroom radios, music boxes, mannequins in small desks, PA crackling.
- **Layout:** playground → main hallway (lockable doors) → classrooms → gym → basement power room → boss arena: the auditorium stage.
- **Objectives:** reach the power room, cut the power, get key part 2 from the principal's office.
- **Key items:** Key part 2, Lily's drawing (spare condition), evidence #3–4.
- **Elena's clue:** "In the dark she only looks for Lily. Use that."
- **Minigame:** lockpicking (lockers, principal's office).
- **Escape routes:** air ducts, the gym's rope climb to the roof, the playground tunnel slide.

### 4.3 St. Agnes Clinic — Owner: **Julian**
- **Atmosphere:** sterile white turning to rot, flickering lights, gurneys, security monitors, green gas.
- **Layout:** reception → wards (long corridors with cameras) → pharmacy → surgery → roof HVAC room → boss arena: surgery hall with glass walls.
- **Objectives:** find the gas mask, reverse the ventilation, retrieve key part 3 and Sam's file.
- **Key items:** Key part 3, gas mask (gear), Sam's file (spare condition), evidence #5–6, medkits.
- **Elena's clue:** "He's asthmatic. The vents are on the roof."
- **Minigame:** lockpicking (pharmacy cabinet — medkit rewards).
- **Escape routes:** laundry chute, morgue freezer (hide, but cold drains stamina), fire stairs.

### 4.4 Church & Cemetery — Owner: **Marcus**
- **Atmosphere:** candles, empty pews with photos of the lost, a choir robe with Clara's name, the bell tower, rain on the graves.
- **Layout:** cemetery (graves, mausoleums for hiding) → nave → crypt → bell tower (vertical boss arena with 3 bell platforms).
- **Objectives:** witness Elena's execution (scripted), survive, fight or evade Marcus, get the chapel key.
- **Key items:** Chapel key, Elena's quarry map, Clara's photo (spare condition, in the choir room), evidence #7–8.
- **Elena's clue (last):** "Marcus hears everything. Make the bells scream."
- **Escape routes:** crypt tunnels (connect to the cemetery), mausoleums, the bell ropes.

### 4.5 Harbor & Lighthouse — Owner: **Elena**
- **Atmosphere (Act I–II):** foggy docks, fishing boats chained, lighthouse beam, warm and safe‑ish because Elena "patrols" it by protecting the player.
- **Atmosphere (Act III):** lighthouse dark, her rain jacket on the chair, radio on static, the group's marks painted over her moth symbols.
- **Layout:** fishing dock → boathouse → lighthouse (spiral stairs) → radio room.
- **Objectives:** Act II meeting with Elena. Act III: grief scene, collect her evidence, optional broadcast (hidden ending).
- **Key items:** Elena's recorder, evidence #9–10, the transmitter (code 1413).
- **Minigames:** **fishing** at the dock; **radio tuning** in the radio room.
- **Threat:** no owner hunter after her death, but in Act III a **roaming hunter** (the next surviving member) visits during blackouts.

### 4.6 Quarry & Veil's Chapel (finale) — Owner: **The Shepherd**
- **Atmosphere:** stone pit, lanterns from the festival hung like trophies, cages, the chapel carved into rock.
- **Layout:** quarry road → cranes and rock paths → holding cells (Lucía, Tomás if hidden ending) → chapel → tunnel gate control.
- **Objectives:** use the chapel key, find Lucía, confront The Shepherd, open the tunnel gate.
- **Key items:** The Shepherd's ledger (lore; the broadcast must already have been sent before entering, see hidden ending).

## 6. Level design rules
- Every zone must have **at least 3 hiding spot types** (lockers/closets, under beds/tables, tall grass/shadows) and **2 escape routes** back to the hub.
- Every hunter arena must allow **fight, spare and flee** (a clearly visible escape exit that opens after a few seconds of the fight).
- Doors marked with the Veil's Eye = a hunter has been there; marked doors are a readable signal for danger.
- Moth symbols = Elena's safe paths and safe rooms.
- Lighting guides the player: warm light = safe, cold/blue = hunter territory, red = active chase.
