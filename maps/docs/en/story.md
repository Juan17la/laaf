# Story — LAAF

> Working title: **LAAF**. Names of places, people and the organization are placeholders and can be changed freely; keep them consistent across all docs.

## 1. Logline

A stranger searching for their missing sister arrives in **Hollowmere**, an isolated mountain town, and is branded with a mark on the first night. The town is watched by five childhood friends who lost everyone they loved and were forced to serve the organization that killed them. One of the friends secretly helps the player. When the group discovers her, they kill her — and the player must decide whether to **escape**, **fight**, or **kill** their way out.

## 2. Tone and themes

- **Tone:** survival horror with action. Tense, grounded, melancholic. Scares come from being hunted, not from monsters.
- **Themes:** grief turned into obedience; guilt; whether victims who become perpetrators can be forgiven; the cost of survival.
- **Key feeling:** the hunters are not evil for fun. Every encounter should make the player understand *why* they do it, which makes the choice to spare or kill meaningful.

## 3. Setting

**Hollowmere** is a lakeside town in a closed mountain valley. The only bridge collapsed years ago; the only way out is the **Old Pass Tunnel** on the north side, sealed by a gate controlled from the quarry. Phones do not work; the only working communication is short‑wave radio. The town still has around thirty inhabitants who keep their heads down and pay tribute to the organization.

The organization is called **The Veil**. It is never fully explained. It recruits people by breaking them, and it uses the town as a "harvest" point for outsiders who arrive by accident or who are lured in. Its leader on the ground is only ever heard on the radio: **The Shepherd**.

## 4. Backstory — The Night of Lanterns

Three years before the game, Hollowmere held its yearly **Lantern Festival** on the lake shore. Five friends who grew up together — **Elena, Marcus, Owen, Nora and Julian** — were there with their families.

That night The Veil cut the town's power and took the festival. People were dragged out of the crowd in the dark. By dawn:

| Friend | Who they lost |
|---|---|
| Elena Ruiz | Her younger brother, Tomás |
| Marcus Hale | His wife, Clara |
| Owen Clarke | Both parents, burned in the sawmill |
| Nora Vance | Her 6‑year‑old daughter, Lily |
| Julian Park | His fiancée, Sam |

The five survivors were brought to the quarry chapel. The Shepherd gave them one choice: **serve or join the dead**. They were branded with **the Veil's Eye** — a spiral with an open eye in the center — on the back of the hand. Since then each of them guards one part of town and marks newcomers.

The Shepherd told them one more thing: *"If you serve well, maybe the ones you lost are not all gone."* This lie (or half‑truth) is what keeps most of them loyal.

## 5. The Mark

- A **marker** (one of the five) paints or burns the Veil's Eye on a victim's door, car, or skin while they sleep.
- A marked person feels watched. The mark "grows" (gameplay: the **Mark meter**).
- At the next **blackout** (the town's power is cut every night at 3:00) the marked are taken to the quarry. They are either broken and recruited, or they disappear.
- The group recruits more people this way — the organization grows through them.

## 6. The player — Alex Moreno

- An outsider (gender is player‑neutral; the name can be changed).
- Came to Hollowmere following the last postcard of their sister **Lucía**, a photographer who stopped answering three weeks ago.
- Car breaks down at the collapsed bridge. Spends the night at the **Lakeview Motel**. Wakes up with the Veil's Eye burned on the back of the hand.

## 7. The traitor — Elena Ruiz

Elena never accepted the deal. She believes Tomás is dead and that The Shepherd lies. She has spent three years collecting **evidence** (photos, recordings, ledgers) of what The Veil does, hidden around town, and she runs a hidden radio in the **Lighthouse**.

When she sees the player marked, she decides to use them: an outsider with nothing to lose who can move around the town. She contacts Alex through notes and radio as **"Moth"** and later meets them in person. She explains:

- what the mark is and how the blackout works;
- who each member is, what they lost, and their weakness;
- that the gate of the Old Pass Tunnel opens with a **three‑part key** held by Owen, Nora and Julian, and that the Veil's Chapel in the quarry opens only with **Marcus's chapel key**;
- that Lucía was taken to the quarry — and may still be alive.

Her death is the midpoint twist of the story.

## 8. Plot structure

> The playable split is **3 chapters × 10 min** — see [chapters.md](chapters.md): Ch.1 *The Mark* = Prologue + Act I + Owen; Ch.2 *Moth* = Act II + Elena's execution; Ch.3 *The Veil* = Act III + Finale, with **The Harvester** as final boss. The act/chapter numbers below are the original long‑form plan.

### Prologue — Arrival
Alex's car stalls at the collapsed bridge. They walk into town at dusk, meet **Grady** (the shopkeeper) who is cold and nervous, and rent a room at the motel. That night, a figure is seen through the window. Fade out.

### Act I — The Mark (Chapters 1–2)
- Alex wakes up marked. The door of the room has the Veil's Eye painted on it.
- First note from "Moth" under the door: *"Don't let them see you after dark. Radio, channel 7."*
- Tutorial of stealth/escape: that night **Owen** hunts Alex through the motel and the edge of the forest.
- Alex reaches the town square safe house. Grady, the neutral merchant, sells supplies for tokens. Minigames open up.
- Radio contact with Moth. She sends Alex to the **Sawmill** to recover the first key part from Owen.

### Act II — The Hunters (Chapters 3–5)
- **Sawmill & Forest (Owen):** traps, chainsaw, fire. First boss choice (spare / kill / flee).
- Alex meets Elena in person for the first time at the harbor. She reveals her identity and the evidence plan.
- **School (Nora)** and **Clinic (Julian)** can be done in any order. Each zone gives a key part or an evidence piece, and a memory of the Night of Lanterns from that member's point of view.
- Relationship with Elena deepens. She tells Alex about Tomás. She asks Alex to help her broadcast the evidence outside the valley.

### Act III — The Traitor (Chapters 6–7)
- Elena sets a meeting at the **Church & Cemetery**, Marcus's zone, to hand over a map of the quarry tunnels she stole from The Shepherd.
- The group was waiting. **Marcus, and whoever of the others is still alive/free**, catch Elena. Alex watches from hiding (or arrives too late — never preventable). **Elena is executed by the group**. Her last words give Alex the lighthouse frequency code.
- From now on the hunt is open: members patrol outside their zones, the Mark rises faster, blackouts are longer.
- Boss fight vs **Marcus** in the bell tower.
- Alex goes to the **Lighthouse**, Elena's now‑empty zone. Grief scene. Optional: broadcast Elena's evidence (requires all evidence).

### Finale — The Quarry (Chapter 8)
- Alex takes Marcus's chapel key and enters **the Veil's Chapel** in the quarry, where Lucía is held. (The Old Pass Tunnel gate next to it opens with the three key parts.)
- Every surviving/spared member appears depending on earlier choices (as enemies, or as allies if spared).
- The Shepherd is revealed to be a tired old man with a radio — the power of The Veil was always the obedience of the broken.
- Final choice and ending.

## 9. Endings

| Ending | Condition | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| **Run** (Escape) | Open the Old Pass Tunnel gate with the 3 key parts and **leave without entering the chapel**. Available as soon as the 3 parts are held — key parts can be stolen by stealth while fleeing boss fights | Alex drives out alone through the tunnel. Lucía is not found. The mark on the hand keeps itching. Radio static: "Channel 7…" Bittersweet. |
| **Unveiled** (Fight / Mercy) | All 4 hunters **spared** | The spared members turn on The Shepherd. The marks are cut from the town's doors. Alex leaves with Lucía. The friends stay to rebuild. Good ending. |
| **The New Eye** (Kill) | All 4 hunters **killed** | Alex reaches the chapel covered in blood. The Shepherd smiles and offers Alex the brand iron: "You did their work better than they did." Final shot: Alex marking a door. Dark ending. |
| **Mixed** | Any other combination | Alex escapes with Lucía, wounded. Epilogue cards tell what happened to each member (spared = redeemed, killed = buried, fled = still guarding the town). |
| **Signal** (Hidden) | All **12 evidence pieces** + broadcast from the lighthouse + no member killed | The broadcast reaches outside the valley. Rescue arrives. Tomás is found alive among the recruited. Elena's name is the last thing on screen. True ending. |

## 10. Narrative delivery

- **Radio calls** (Elena, later The Shepherd taunting the player).
- **Notes and evidence** (collectibles, readable in the journal).
- **Memories**: after each boss, a short playable flashback of the Night of Lanterns from that member's view.
- **Environmental storytelling**: marked doors, altars made with festival lanterns, children's drawings in the school, empty chairs in the church.
- **Member dialogue while hunting**: each hunter talks while searching; lines reveal their pain and doubts.

## 11. What the Veil was (revealed in C3.4, after the ending)

The Veil is never an ancient cult — it is one man's grief with a town's guilt around it.

- **The Shepherd** is **Elias Morrow**, Hollowmere's old quarry chaplain.
- Thirty‑one years ago the quarry collapsed on nine men; his son **Caleb** was one. The town voted to seal the pit to keep the pass open ("save the rest" — the same triage Julian repeats).
- On the ninth day Caleb dug himself out, blind to any light and without words. Elias told the valley the pit had *kept* him, that it keeps everyone if it is fed, and turned his son into its executioner: **The Harvester** (red miner's‑lamp lenses, pitch from the pit, a sword of the nine he couldn't save and everyone since).
- **The 03:00 blackout** exists because light hurts Caleb. That is also why light is the Harvester's weakness.
- The Taken who broke were shipped out in the Tuesday trucks (Elena's recording, evidence #9); the others stayed in the pit.
- **Pop. 1413** on the town sign is Hollowmere's population the year the pit fell. Elena chose it as her transmitter code: the one number the Shepherd couldn't make smaller.
- The Shepherd's line in C3.3 — *"I never took anyone. They brought them to me."* — is true: the town and the five friends did the taking.
